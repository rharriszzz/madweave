Hex Weave & Mad Weave – An Introduction to Triaxial Weaving, by Elizabeth Harris and Charlene St. John

The original MadWeaver site introduced this book under the working title Handbook of Triaxial Weaves. Schiffer published it as Hex Weave & Mad Weave, ISBN 9780764344657. Publisher’s book page

The authors use diagrams, photographs and thirteen projects to show how strips interlace in three directions. Projects range from paper stars to bags and a table runner. The pattern and color exercises suit individual study and workshops.

To try a color pattern on screen, open MadWeaver. The illustrated instructions explain how to paint a strip and follow it through a crossing.
